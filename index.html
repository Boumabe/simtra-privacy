Simtra – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Simtra is an educational trading simulator. You learn technical analysis level by level using virtual money. This policy explains what information Simtra handles and how it is used.

1. Information we collect

Simtra does not currently require an account. We do not ask for, and do not collect, your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos or payment information.

To make the app work, Simtra uses Google Firebase. Firebase may process technical information such as an app installation identifier, device type, operating system version and IP address. We use this information only to run, store data for, and maintain the app.

2. Data stored by the app

Simtra stores app data (such as lessons, levels and, where applicable, your progress: virtual balance, levels completed, stars, XP, rank, achievements, streak and settings) using Google Firebase and on your device. This data is used only to provide the features of the app.

3. Virtual money only

All money in Simtra is virtual. Simtra does not handle real money, does not connect to brokerage accounts and does not process payments.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell or rent your information. The only third-party service provider we use is Google (Firebase), which processes data on our behalf to store and deliver app data. You can read Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Future accounts

We plan to add account registration and sign-in in a future version. If we do, we may collect information such as your email address to create and secure your account. Before this happens, we will update this policy and the date above.

6. Data retention and deletion

You can delete the data stored on your device by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. To request deletion of data stored on our servers, contact us at the email below.

7. Children

Simtra is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Not financial advice

Simtra is for educational purposes only. Nothing in the app is financial, investment or trading advice. Results in the simulator do not predict real market results.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the date of the last update shown above.
